Motif Allostery Summary
=======================

Excitation convention:
Z[t,i] = -1 (excited), Z[t,i] = +1 (not excited)

Definitions:
Residue source support cutoff: #{t : Z[t,j] == -1} >= 20
Motif regions are structural dimer/trimer regions.
Dimers are peptide-bonded residue pairs.
Peptide trimers are consecutive peptide-bonded triples.
Triangulated triads contain at least one peptide edge and at least one non-covalent edge.
Region coupling aggregation mode: mean (topk=5)
Directional region coupling: C_region[A,B] = aggregate(D_res weighted by motif memberships)
Symmetric affinity:
Affinity[A,B] = |C[A,B]| + |C[B,A]|, then robustly scaled and clipped to [0,1]
Affinity scale value used: 0.009020338444
